# Halferne Series: Master Timeline

> Scope: shared reference for the twelve Halferne novels. Initial coverage is limited to the drafted text of *The Halferne Deception*, reviewed 2026-10-02; other books have not yet been incorporated. Sources below refer to files in [03-The-Halferne-Deception](https://github.com/djaysnider/03-The-Halferne-Deception), not outline chapter numbers. Future entries should identify both the book repository and source file. These are living reference notes, not a declaration that every first-draft detail is final canon. Character claims, author notes, interpretations, and unresolved conflicts are labeled separately. No facts from other books or unapproved brainstorming have been imported.

## Reading the Timeline

* No absolute calendar date is established in the reviewed material.
* Sequence is stronger than exact elapsed time: retain relative anchors and mark contradictions.
* Repository order does not guarantee event order, especially around the escape and safehouse.
* Empty files 19 and 25–32 provide no completed events.

## Safehouse Sequence — Placement Unresolved

21 Safe House currently supplies this sequence:

1. Full crew assembles at a solarium; Krisp is alive and present.
2. Tiron confronts Bix about abandoning him.
3. The case is opened and a compact machine issues warnings.
4. A broadcast identifies crew members.
5. Bix splits the crew into groups and takes Krisp and Luuk with him.
6. Pursuit begins and a hauler spills cargo; the scene stops mid-action.

This cannot be inserted straightforwardly between 20 Escape and 22 Krisp's End: the latter returns to the immediate extraction, places Krisp with Poppy and Alayn, and leads directly to his death. Preserve the safehouse as an unresolved draft sequence until its intended placement or replacement is decided.
