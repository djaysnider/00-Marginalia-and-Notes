# Halferne Series: Character Bible

> Scope: shared reference for the twelve Halferne novels. Current coverage: *The Halferne Deception* (reviewed 2026-10-02), *The Halferne Perfidy* (added 2026-10-05), *The Halferne Expedition* (added 2026-10-11), *Tales from Russo’s* (added 2026-10-11), and *The Malyon Gambit* (added 2026-10-11). Reorganized 2026-10-06 into one alphabetical character register across sources. These are living reference notes; character claims, author notes, interpretations, and unresolved conflicts remain distinct from established canon.

## Maintenance and sorting

* Sort by surname, then given name; ignore titles such as Dr., Marshal, and Chancellor. Keep surname particles as written: **de Boer** under D and **van Rijn** under V.
* Single names and entities without established surnames sort under their recorded name. **Yogananda Rimpoche** is sorted under Y because Rimpoche is a religious title, not an established family name. Kathlyn’s surname is not inferred from her father.
* Keep aliases with the same character. Cross-book evidence belongs inside the character’s entry; it must not create a second person or overwrite an earlier book’s knowledge limits.
* Add or revise entries when the manuscript changes or the author explicitly adopts a decision. Preserve unresolved alternatives rather than silently choosing one.
* Record skills separately from motives, beliefs, and relationships; identify whether future developments are written or merely planned.
* Source numbers without links in Deception entries refer to manuscript files in [03-The-Halferne-Deception](https://github.com/djaysnider/03-The-Halferne-Deception), not outline chapter numbers. Perfidy sources link to [01-The-Halferne-Perfidy](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy). Expedition sources link to [04-The-Halferne-Expedition](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition). Tales sources link to [13-Tales-From-Russos](https://github.com/djaysnider/13-Tales-From-Russos). Malyon sources link to [07-The-Malyon-Gambit](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit). Numbering may change during revision.

## Amin, Umar

**Source context: The Halferne Expedition**

**Role and appearance:** Expedition geologist; portly, middle-aged, usually in coveralls. Socially forthcoming, absorbed in his work, and acquainted with Clarc from the charter voyage.

**Capabilities and behavior:** At ease in caves despite his anxiety elsewhere. Designs the five-point tunnel-collapse plan, reads the ice/atmosphere hazards, and contributes isotope and geological hypotheses about the disk. His expertise is distinct from his tendency to panic during encounters with unfamiliar life and technology.

**Relationships and trajectory:** Reveals to Jaysn that Clarc’s father was an SI, while refusing to tell her private history for her. When wisps surround him, Wolff comes to protect him; a transport cube forms around the two men and Clarc follows on its roof. The three subsequently reach the humanoid village.

**Fate:** Villagers force him into contact with the obelisk; he is disassembled and incorporated into the city wall. An attempted rollback briefly shows a chance to rescue him, but it does not become his final outcome. His death is acknowledged by the surviving party.

**Sources:** [05 Tamana](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/05%20Tamana.md); [06 Galley](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/06%20Galley.md); [07 Briefing](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/07%20Briefing.md); [09 Confined](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/09%20Confined.md); [10 Descent](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/10%20Descent.md); [15 Arch](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/15%20Arch.md); [17 Gallery](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/17%20Gallery.md); [23 Ceremony](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/23%20Ceremony.md); [28 Time Rift](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/28%20Time%20Rift.md); [36 Meet the Reclamation](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/36%20Meet%20the%20Reclamation.md).

## Baddon, Rikkard (Rik)

**Source context: The Halferne Deception**

**Role and background**

* Central protagonist; transport mechanic, security specialist, and part-owner of B.D.D. Repairs.
* Grew up in social services with Krisp; the two live in adjacent apartments on Level Seven of Arco Korab.
* Qualified for manual labor and Grade Seven mechanic work; Krisp refers to him as a Grade Nine mechanic in their present life.
* Nickname/reputation: “The Mechanic,” recognized by Tiron.

**Capabilities**

* Plans the Clerc theft around deliberate transport-regulator miscalibration.
* Understands biometric locks, surveillance, vehicle maintenance, and practical escape preparation.
* Has experience in childhood brawls and lane skipping; does not regard himself as a strong combat specialist.
* Displays discomfort in exposed open sky despite familiarity with heights inside the arco.

**Values and behavior**

* Insists on honest treatment of repair customers while participating in theft outside the shop.
* Is reluctant to commit to career crime.
* Accepts a beating intended for Krisp and conceals the debt from Lora.
* Promises not to abandon teammates and returns to rescue Tiron.
* After Krisp's death, seeks Thoughtbridge as leverage against arrest and corporate retaliation.

**Relationships**

* Krisp: lifelong friend and chosen brother; affection mixed with divergent ambitions.
* Lora: business partner; respects her but finds her confrontational.
* Tiron: distrust develops into practical cooperation and a demonstrated rescue.
* Bix: crew leader whose plan and accountability Rik increasingly questions.
* Poppy: mutual friction between caution and exuberance.

**Sources:** 03 Clerc; 04 Mechanic; 07 Rik and Krisp; 08 Rik and Lora; 09 Intro Wart; 10 Intro Alayn; 15 Rik and Poppy; 16 Rehearsal; 20 Escape; 24 Vyn's.

**Source context: Tales from Russo’s**

**Six weeks after the siege:** Takes responsibility for the surviving Aurian Embassy on Midway, with Lutz arranging the lease and Nichols formally recognizing his administration. Emil accepts Tiron’s suggested “Chief” address. Rik proposes food, shelter, medical support, and resettlement for refugees, then whatever further action allies and resources permit.

**Motivation and relationships:** Tiron presses him about the missing two years, the royal connection, and apparent abandonment of their old self-interest. Rik describes knowing the king personally and witnessing him die while trying to save a stranger. Treats that example as a duty to help where he can, rather than solely a political allegiance.

**Later wartime state:** In Wine, Women, and Song, watches Notosia burn without satisfaction, withdraws from the gestalt, and mourns the grounding influence Tiron once supplied. Jaysn helps him connect and reassures him about the value of their combined abilities. Cwen arrives at the end. During Fists and Feet, insists Ursza use fists and feet instead of concealed weapons and avoid attracting trouble on the Embassy’s steps.

**Memory caution:** Access to Serah’s memories and Ursza’s skills is explicit in these later stories. It must not be backdated into his earlier Deception profile.

**Sources:** [04 Purpose and Promise](https://github.com/djaysnider/13-Tales-From-Russos/blob/main/04%20Purpose%20and%20Promise.md); [05 Wine, Women, and Song](https://github.com/djaysnider/13-Tales-From-Russos/blob/main/05%20Wine%2C%20Women%2C%20and%20Song.md); [07 Fists and Feet](https://github.com/djaysnider/13-Tales-From-Russos/blob/main/07%20Fists%20and%20Feet.md).

**Source context: The Malyon Gambit**

After the earlier shop troubles, smuggles Cort with Tiron and encounters Sal in the undercity. Kills Wart during the attack on them. Accepts the King’s offer to command the Royal Order of the Heart after Haravan’s assassination, despite class resentment, friends’ objections, and his own uncertainty about responsibility.

**Capabilities and choices:** Reads people and pressure situations quickly, questions ritual security procedures, wins Helena’s first infiltration challenge, and chooses to help compromised Ibarra by paying his debt rather than discarding him. Recognizes the incoming false transport by its dead soldiers’ names. Repairs the damaged escaping shuttle under extreme heat and electrical injury; the King pulls him clear and Helena restores controlled flight.

**Continuity:** Opening uses Rickard, later Rikkard. The Wart attack is on sublevel twenty here versus sublevel five in Tiron’s Tales recollection. These are source discrepancies, not separate Rik/Sal encounters established by the register.

**Sources:** [03 (Intro Rik)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/03%20%28Intro%20Rik%29.md); [06 (Rik and Sal)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/06%20%28Rik%20and%20Sal%29.md); [08 (Serah and Delanna)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/08%20%28Serah%20and%20Delanna%29.md); [11 (Rik’s Debate)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/11%20%28Rik%E2%80%99s%20Debate%29.md); [14 (Reception)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/14%20%28Reception%29.md); [16 (Serah’s Break Into Two)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/16%20%28Serah%E2%80%99s%20Break%20Into%20Two%29.md); [18 (Enter Helena)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/18%20%28Enter%20Helena%29.md); [19 (Planetfall)](https://github.com/djaysnider/07-The-Malyon-Gambit/blob/main/19%20%28Planetfall%29.md).

## Clarc, Cari

**Source context: The Halferne Expedition**

**Role and appearance:** Expedition xenozoologist; short-cropped black hair, visible head/neck cybernetics, tracheal and neural enhancements, and a high-quality left-arm prosthesis. Guarded, blunt, and often hostile to synthetic intelligences. Describes herself as agnostic and worries about the implications of a species capable of shepherding or destroying other intelligent life.

**Capabilities:** Interprets alien behavior, sensory biology, and possible argon-world biochemistry. Climbs onto the departing cube to stay with Amin and Wolff, participates in the light-pattern exchange, and later decodes the villagers’ chest-beating/directional gestures. Physically capable and willing to fight, but initially argues against assuming the humanoids are violent.

**Background and relationships:** Her biological father died in a containment breach on Beta Tangra when she was four. Her mother hired an SI to impersonate him through remote holos, telling Cari he was working off-world. Cari discovered the deception at sixteen while applying for university and finding her deceased father’s educational trust. The SI claimed genuine affection and said he returned his fees to that trust; these motives are his account as relayed by Cari. The betrayal explains her distrust of SIs, rather than proving all SIs behave similarly.

**Written trajectory:** Challenges Novik’s decisions about Tamana and Wolff, then hears his defense of their agency. Holds Wolff’s sidearm during Novik’s transfer into the Overseer role, authorized to stop him if he becomes dangerous. Returns alive with Jaysn and Solvig and resumes contact with Syn. Thomas Clarc, introducing him as her father.

**Memory and future:** Her detailed recollections begin fading like a dream; notes contain sequence errors Jaysn corrects. Declines immediately building a career on the dead colleagues’ experience, considers action against Ravenhold/Halferne, and leaves later collaboration open.

**Body caution:** The simulated city form temporarily replaces her prosthetic arm with a biological one while retaining other enhancements. Do not treat that adaptation as a permanent physical change after returning.

**Sources:** [05 Tamana](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/05%20Tamana.md); [06 Galley](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/06%20Galley.md); [13 Platform](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/13%20Platform.md); [15 Arch](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/15%20Arch.md); [17 Gallery](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/17%20Gallery.md); [21 Raptor](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/21%20Raptor.md); [23 Ceremony](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/23%20Ceremony.md); [31 Moses](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/31%20Moses.md); [32 Clarc's Past](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/32%20Clarc's%20Past.md); [34 Reclamation Talk](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/34%20Reclamation%20Talk.md); [35 Overseer](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/35%20Overseer.md); [37 Halferne](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/37%20Halferne.md); [39 Clarc](https://github.com/djaysnider/04-The-Halferne-Expedition/blob/main/39%20Clarc.md).

## Clarc, Thomas (Syn.)

**Source context: The Halferne Expedition**

Synthetic intelligence whom Cari introduces as her father during a holo conversation aboard the returning starliner. Presented as a graying, late-middle-aged man in a plain worker’s tunic, with mischievous eyes; offers condolences to Jaysn.

**Identity and relationship:** The SI father figure connected to Cari’s childhood deception and later estrangement. **Evidence caution:** His claims that he cared for the family and returned the impersonation fees to Cari’s educational trust are relayed in her account. His physical appearance here is a holo presentation.

## Clay, Sean (alias: Alexandre Girard)

**Source context: The Halferne Perfidy**

**Role and background:** Sol Directorate Division 5 operative and principal viewpoint character. Uses Alexandre Girard of Procyon’s Bonai Saigo as his business cover; ‘Alex’ is used during the pilgrimage. Has decades of field experience.

**Capabilities:** Enhanced optical scanning/HUD, neural interface, trained hand-to-hand combat, surveillance, deception, and improvised escape. Takes Karim’s tracker and uses it through the operation. Experience does not protect him from repeatedly misreading Notosian loyalties.

**Motives and relationships:** Initially prioritizes the Keraunos mission and Directorate interests. His unresolved grief over daughter Kathlyn informs his response to Ursza’s coercion. Ness is his assigned local partner and later betrays him to Cerberus. Prudence draws out the daughter’s history; Halferne uses his protective instincts.

**Written trajectory:** Kills Hoefer and Talbot; is captured and tortured by Cerberus. Saleh rescues him. Ultimately abandons the cipher to save Halferne from Ursza’s conditioned command, then leaves the system with Halferne. Survives the book.

**Evidence caution:** The final debrief labels all objectives complete, but is an administrative reinterpretation of the operation. Do not equate the report with Clay knowingly following that plan.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md); [12 Debate](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/12%20Debate.md); [14 Prudence](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/14%20Prudence.md); [34 The End](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/34%20The%20End.md); [39 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Denouement.md); [41 Pilatus](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/41%20Pilatus.md).

**Source context: Tales from Russo’s**

**Later visit — four months before liberation:** Retired from Division 5 the previous year; travels to Midway armed and prepared for a confrontation with Serah. Questions her responsibility as the second Hēi Gēzi and his own decision to spare Ursza and surrender Keraunos ten years earlier. He does not arrive with a fully settled intention to kill her, though he is prepared to use the weapon and die.

**Resolution and relationships:** Serah directs him toward the living Ursza, disproving his belief she had been killed. Ursza returns his ring after keeping it from the night following their final Excipio encounter. She says his choice made Serah’s eventual rescue of her possible and accepts responsibility for the subsequent struggle with Serah. Clay regains a sense that he has things left to do and urges them to allow themselves that same possibility.

**Evidence caution:** His attribution of multiple wars/atrocities to Serah and his fear they are his own fault are the confrontation’s accusations and guilt, not an independently verified causal history. Serah and Ursza’s later mental discussion shows they deliberately spare him a fuller account of Halferne’s manipulation while aiming to remain honest.

**Sources:** [06 After the Labyrinth](https://github.com/djaysnider/13-Tales-From-Russos/blob/main/06%20After%20the%20Labyrinth.md).

## Overseer — original alien custodian

**Source context: The Halferne Expedition**

**Identity and function:** Biological custodian of the simulated world/solar system held in the disk. Distinct from its creators and from Lev Novik, who later assumes his functions. Solvig’s interface account describes him as the last of his kind, waiting hundreds of millions of years for people who never arrived.

**Presentations:** Each visitor initially perceives an image suited to their frame of reference: Solvig’s dazzling light, Clarc’s predator, Wolff’s uniformed authority, Novik’s mathematical abstraction, Tamana’s mother tree, and Jaysn’s shifting theatrical mask. The later chamber reveals a squid-like body with a mushroom-like upper mass and tentacles. Those descriptions are human perceptions, not a settled species name.

**Capabilities and constraints:** Communicates by interpreting minds and metaphors, with Jaysn as his easiest human primer. Maintains simulated natural law, briefly pauses/rolls back events, and cannot freely undo everything once witnessed. Influences the evolved humanoids into rebuilding the city through the obelisk. After Tamana frees them from that control, initiates destructive nanoparticle rain to preserve the larger design.

**Motives and outcome:** Frames destruction as mercy and protection of the simulation; the party disputes that judgment. The wider account links the archive to refuge from the Reclamation. Hands over functions through the light-bee interface when Novik volunteers. His body goes limp and the interface breaks; the text does not explicitly confirm his biological death.

**Evidence caution:** Separate his own justifications and Solvig’s translated information from directly observed events. Omnipotence, universal knowledge, and godhood are not established.
