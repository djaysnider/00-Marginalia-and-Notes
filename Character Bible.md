# Halferne Series: Character Bible

> Scope: shared reference for the twelve Halferne novels. Coverage includes the drafted text of *The Halferne Deception* (reviewed 2026-10-02) and *The Halferne Perfidy* (added 2026-10-05); other books have not yet been incorporated. The original Deception entries below refer to files in [03-The-Halferne-Deception](https://github.com/djaysnider/03-The-Halferne-Deception), not outline chapter numbers. Future entries should identify both the book repository and source file. These are living reference notes, not a declaration that every first-draft detail is final canon. Character claims, author notes, interpretations, and unresolved conflicts are labeled separately. No facts from other books or unapproved brainstorming have been imported.

## Maintenance

* Add or revise entries when the manuscript changes or the author explicitly adopts a decision.
* Preserve unresolved alternatives rather than silently choosing one.
* Record skills separately from motives, beliefs, and relationships.
* For future developments, identify whether they are written or merely planned.

## Rik / Rikkard Baddon

**Role and background**

* Central protagonist; transport mechanic, security specialist, and part-owner of B.D.D. Repairs.
* Grew up in social services with Krisp; the two live in adjacent apartments on Level Seven of Arco Korab.
* Qualified for manual labor and Grade Seven mechanic work; Krisp refers to him as a Grade Nine mechanic in their present life.
* Nickname/reputation: “The Mechanic,” recognized by Tiron.

**Capabilities**

* Plans the Clerc theft around deliberate transport-regulator miscalibration.
* Understands biometric locks, surveillance, vehicle maintenance, and practical escape preparation.
* Has experience in childhood brawls and lane skipping; does not regard himself as a strong combat specialist.
* Displays discomfort in exposed open sky despite familiarity with heights inside the arco.

**Values and behavior**

* Insists on honest treatment of repair customers while participating in theft outside the shop.
* Is reluctant to commit to career crime.
* Accepts a beating intended for Krisp and conceals the debt from Lora.
* Promises not to abandon teammates and returns to rescue Tiron.
* After Krisp's death, seeks Thoughtbridge as leverage against arrest and corporate retaliation.

**Relationships**

* Krisp: lifelong friend and chosen brother; affection mixed with divergent ambitions.
* Lora: business partner; respects her but finds her confrontational.
* Tiron: distrust develops into practical cooperation and a demonstrated rescue.
* Bix: crew leader whose plan and accountability Rik increasingly questions.
* Poppy: mutual friction between caution and exuberance.

**Sources:** 03 Clerc; 04 Mechanic; 07 Rik and Krisp; 08 Rik and Lora; 09 Intro Wart; 10 Intro Alayn; 15 Rik and Poppy; 16 Rehearsal; 20 Escape; 24 Vyn's.

## Krisp / Krispin Dijek

**Role and background**

* Mechanic, hardware improviser, and B.D.D. Repairs partner.
* Raised at St. Edmund School; regards Dr. Laurent as his father and Rik as his brother.
* Declined an engineering opportunity partly to remain with Rik.
* Chose not to learn the available details of his biological family.

**Capabilities**

* Builds effective replacement parts from salvaged machinery.
* Creates the interface connecting Daria's Earth implant to local systems.
* Diagnoses false transport telemetry and removes hardware to defeat remote interference.
* Experienced lane skipper.

**Motives and beliefs**

* Wants more opportunity than routine mechanical work provides.
* Values staying with Rik and remaining on Auria.
* Is attracted to Daria but declines her invitation to leave the world.
* In his final scene, interprets the universe deterministically and resists defining himself as a criminal.

**Fate in drafted text**

* Takes Thoughtbridge away from Poppy's compromised transport.
* Falls while escaping pursuit; safety beams and gravnet fail to arrest his descent.
* Death is confirmed by Alayn's observation in 23 Betrayal.
* Blames Daria in his final thoughts; the precise control path behind the safety failures is not independently explained.

**Conflicts to resolve**

* 22 Krisp's End gives his age as 24; the broadcast in 21 Safe House gives 29.
* 07 Rik and Krisp mentions a Grade Fourteen engineering position; 18 Into Vantaris identifies Level Fifteen engineering aptitude. These may describe different measures.
* 21 Safe House places him with the full crew in a sequence that conflicts with the adjacent escape/death narrative.

**Sources:** 04 Mechanic; 07 Rik and Krisp; 13 Krisp and Daria; 18 Into Vantaris; 21 Safe House; 22 Krisp's End; 23 Betrayal.

## Tiron Essien

**Role**

* Confidence and infiltration specialist recruited from Ghaffari's competing operation.
* Has cultivated Clerc's trust before Bix's crew steals the prototype.
* Lives on Level Nine before Tesnica freezes his assets and removes his possessions.

**Capabilities**

* Reads social cues, impersonates expected roles, manipulates card deals, and improvises cover stories.
* Uses a concealed sleeve-mounted weapon.
* Commissions a kudzu linked to Oskar Hol and uses it to influence Bethir.
* Assumes practical tactical leadership when the heist fails.

**Motives and relationships**

* Needs the job's proceeds to escape Ghaffari's financial leverage.
* Distrusts teammates and evaluates loyalties through incentives.
* Recognizes Rik's sincerity even while doubting his judgment.
* Vijay Khanna provides identity assistance and temporary work/refuge.
* Clerc retaliates after discovering his manipulation.

**Sources:** 05 Tiron and Bix; 06 Tiron and Clerc; 08 Rik and Lora; 14 Tiron and Alayn; 16 Rehearsal; 17 The Party; 18 Into Vantaris; 20 Escape.

## Stev Bix

* Leader of the heist crew; recruiter and presenter of the operational plan.
* Charming and confident when the plan is working.
* Recruits Tiron while arranging to redirect Tesnica's investigation.
* Protests that he does not improvise when Tiron demands a diversion.
* Participates in the betrayal with Daria and Luuk, confirmed in 23 Betrayal.
* Tiron alleges a romantic or manipulative relationship with Daria; the allegation should not be treated as independently established.
* The identity of any alternative patron is not established in the drafted text.

**Sources:** 03 Clerc; 05 Tiron and Bix; 11 Assemble; 12 The Ask; 16 Rehearsal; 18 Into Vantaris; 20 Escape; 23 Betrayal; 24 Vyn's.

## Daria Sokolova

* Slicer with legitimate Earth neural augmentation, normally concealed by a wig.
* Natural hair is short orange with shaved sides; the neural jack is behind her left ear.
* Has subconscious memory implants supporting rapid mathematical/encryption work.
* Wants to leave Auria, finish her education, and understand the technology she uses.
* Says she rejected permanent Phrame dependence on an Earth corporation.
* Invites Krisp to leave with her; her sincerity is disputed by Tiron.
* Participates in the betrayal and acknowledges Alayn with a mocking wave as she leaves.
* Broadcast description in 21 Safe House gives age 27; treat broadcast details as reported information.

**Sources:** 11 Assemble; 12 The Ask; 13 Krisp and Daria; 16 Rehearsal; 18 Into Vantaris; 23 Betrayal.

## Luuk Smit

* Physically imposing member of Bix's existing crew.
* Can appear shy and careful in social settings.
* Performs the prototype substitution in the Clerc theft.
* Uses nonlethal plaser fire to neutralize command-center guards.
* Accompanies Bix and Daria during the betrayal.
* Retrieves Thoughtbridge from Krisp's remains.
* His private motives and degree of independence from Bix remain unestablished.

**Sources:** 03 Clerc; 11 Assemble; 18 Into Vantaris; 20 Escape; 23 Betrayal.

## Poppy Patel

* Driver recruited for her record of racing and evading a coordinated pursuit.
* Comes from the open-air levels; unfamiliar with some arco conventions.
* Excited by freedom, speed, and the possibility of substantial earnings.
* Describes death as an end to worries and regrets.
* Skilled at manual maneuvering and disabling a transponder without stopping the engine.
* Extracts Krisp and Alayn; later leaves Alayn to attempt recovery of the case.
* Knows Techlan in the Baxter province and proposes it as a rendezvous.

**Sources:** 11 Assemble; 15 Rik and Poppy; 22 Krisp's End; 23 Betrayal.

## Alayn de Boer

* Demolition/chemical specialist, long dark hair tied back.
* Recognizes concussion symptoms from practical first-aid knowledge.
* Admits he has never attempted a job like the Vantaris heist.
* Conceals his privileged identity until Vyn prompts its discovery.
* Heir to the De Boer Diagnostics fortune; Rik describes him as a highly credentialed prodigy.
* Refuses to involve his family in recovering Thoughtbridge.
* Witnesses Krisp's death and the betrayers' departure.
* Challenges Vyn directly about his advance knowledge of the job.

**Name conflict:** “Landsman” and “Fielder” appear before the de Boer reveal. Landsman is explicitly connected to the concealed identity in 24 Vyn's; whether Fielder is another intentional alias or a draft remnant is unresolved.

**Sources:** 10 Intro Alayn; 11 Assemble; 12 The Ask; 14 Tiron and Alayn; 20 Escape; 23 Betrayal; 24 Vyn's.

## Bethir / Beithir

* Vantaris security intelligence; controls or overrides building systems.
* Identifies crew members through surveillance associations.
* Accepts Tiron's Dr. Hol identity and executes instructions.
* Can act lethally, including dropping the occupied-location lift car into the basement.
* Described during the operation as rogue/corrupted.
* Briefing and operational scenes disagree over whether the crew knew an SI protected the building.
* Name spelling varies; no distinct second entity is established.

**Sources:** 12 The Ask; 18 Into Vantaris; 20 Escape.

## The Halferne Perfidy — Prose Coverage

Reviewed 2026-10-05 against chapters 03–43 in [01-The-Halferne-Perfidy](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy). These entries describe the characters at the time of this novel, not their eventual twelve-book identities or roles. Deaths depicted in a fabricated broadcast are not additional deaths. Author footnotes, cover stories, and character allegations are distinguished from independently narrated events. Names below include off-world characters and referenced relatives, as well as the cast physically on Notosia.

### Sean Clay / Alexandre Girard

**Role and background:** Sol Directorate Division 5 operative and principal viewpoint character. Uses Alexandre Girard of Procyon’s Bonai Saigo as his business cover; ‘Alex’ is used during the pilgrimage. Has decades of field experience.

**Capabilities:** Enhanced optical scanning/HUD, neural interface, trained hand-to-hand combat, surveillance, deception, and improvised escape. Takes Karim’s tracker and uses it through the operation. Experience does not protect him from repeatedly misreading Notosian loyalties.

**Motives and relationships:** Initially prioritizes the Keraunos mission and Directorate interests. His unresolved grief over daughter Kathlyn informs his response to Ursza’s coercion. Ness is his assigned local partner and later betrays him to Cerberus. Prudence draws out the daughter’s history; Halferne uses his protective instincts.

**Written trajectory:** Kills Hoefer and Talbot; is captured and tortured by Cerberus. Saleh rescues him. Ultimately abandons the cipher to save Halferne from Ursza’s conditioned command, then leaves the system with Halferne. Survives the book.

**Evidence caution:** The final debrief labels all objectives complete, but is an administrative reinterpretation of the operation. Do not equate the report with Clay knowingly following that plan.

**Sources:** [03 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/03%20Excipio%20Station.md); [11 Debate](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/11%20Debate.md); [13 Prudence](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/13%20Prudence.md); [33 The End](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/33%20The%20End.md); [38 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/38%20Denouement.md); [40 Pilatus](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/40%20Pilatus.md).

### Marte Ness

**Role and background:** Presented as a private detective and local operative assisting Clay; Notosian-born. Says her grandfather came through work release after serving the Yegorova Network and her father was a policeman.

**Capabilities:** Pilots their transports, supports surveillance and tracking, knows local factions, and employs swift close-range lethal force. Kills Karim at Excipio despite Clay intending to release him.

**Values and relationships:** Argues that there are no civilians or reliable loyalties on Notosia and repeatedly challenges Clay’s protective impulses. Later exposed as serving Cerberus; Clay confronts her before his capture.

**Fate:** Protects Omari during the faction meeting; Ursza decapitates her in 35 The Long Knives. This is an actual narrated death.

**Sources:** [03 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/03%20Excipio%20Station.md); [11 Debate](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/11%20Debate.md); [31 Captured](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/31%20Captured.md); [35 The Long Knives](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/35%20The%20Long%20Knives.md).

### Ursza Venter / Hēi Gēzi

**Role and background:** Gu’Senlin monk from Adad. Came to Notosia intending to help establish a temple, schools, or clinics. Explains that Cerberus recruited/coerced her and made her dependent on Shard.

**Aliases and capabilities:** The supposedly separate assassin Hēi Gēzi is Ursza herself. Skilled in armed/unarmed combat, concealment, climbing and movement, discipline of pain, and navigating Harba City. The later Warmaster title belongs to her in this novel; do not import Serah’s later use of the persona.

**Coercion and motives:** Shard dependency and prior conditioning constrain her choices. Talbot controls supply; Prevo can command her. Her written explanation and Saleh’s account describe her exploiting periods of sanity to plot against both men. Keep medical mechanism and conditioning claims attributed where appropriate.

**Relationships:** Clay tries to rescue her and repeatedly misreads her apparent betrayals. Yogananda’s monks assist her. Halferne seeks to preserve/extract her while manipulating Clay. Saleh coordinates elements of her escape and opposition to Prevo.

**Fate and limits:** Kills Omari and Ness while serving as Prevo’s Warmaster. At Excipio she pursues the cipher and is ordered to kill Halferne if it is not obtained; Clay relinquishes it to save him. Returns to Notosia under Prevo’s control. Halferne still calls her viable in his private conversation with Mother Eye.

**Naming caution:** Ursa and Urza occur alongside Ursza. Marga and Lars Venter are names Halferne invokes in the confrontation; their precise relationship to Ursza is not explained here.

**Sources:** [07 Hostel](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/07%20Hostel.md); [20 Shrine](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/20%20Shrine.md); [28 Gu’Senlin](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/28%20Gu%E2%80%99Senlin.md); [35 The Long Knives](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/35%20The%20Long%20Knives.md); [38 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/38%20Denouement.md); [39 Hello, Mother](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Hello%2C%20Mother.md).

### Abil Halferne / the Raven — Perfidy appearance

**Role:** Named professor/advisor within Prevo’s government; ‘the Raven’ is his covert designation. Describes himself as an advisor rather than an enforcer. The final Directorate report identifies him as an embedded ally.

**Capabilities and behavior:** Knows Clay’s identity, protocols, and private communications; arranges the museum encounter, hostage exchange, and extraction opportunities. Uses surveillance and contingencies while giving partial explanations.

**Relationships and motives:** Ursza’s survival/extraction is a primary concern, explicitly discussed with Mother Eye. Admits he pushed Clay too far by underestimating his honor. Cooperates with Mother Eye and uses Saleh’s assistance; the exact extent of his plan is not independently established.

**Fate:** Clay gives up the cipher to prevent Ursza killing him. Escapes with Clay to Midway. His private conversation confirms his contingencies suffered a setback; it does not establish unlimited foresight.

**Cross-book note:** Expands the previously unseen Halferne contact in Deception’s supporting table. That table’s limited evidence remains valid for that novel.

**Sources:** [22 The Eyes of Baal](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/22%20The%20Eyes%20of%20Baal.md); [23 The Museum](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/23%20The%20Museum.md); [27 The Heist](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/27%20The%20Heist.md); [38 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/38%20Denouement.md); [39 Hello, Mother](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Hello%2C%20Mother.md); [40 Pilatus](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/40%20Pilatus.md).

### Tariq Saleh; Obryn

**Role and identity:** Initially appears as Klein’s red-haired traveling companion Obryn. Reveals himself as Saleh and head of the Notosian Liberation Front. Do not create separate entries for the reveal.

**Capabilities and behavior:** Political maneuvering, faction alliances, infiltration, tactical rescue, and use of propaganda against the regime. Compels Omari to consider a coalition and uses her production resources as leverage.

**Relationships and trajectory:** Breaks with Klein; briefly presents a Cerberus/Noto Lib alliance. Escapes the meeting massacre and extracts Clay after the missile attack. Says the surviving opposition can be rallied against Prevo.

**Evidence caution:** Claims coalition memberships and his control of factions must be dated and attributed. Directorate briefing calls Tariq Saleh an independent operative/alias; actual birth name is not established.

**Naming conflict:** Tarek, Tariq, and Tarak occur; Obryn also appears as Obyrn. Author footnote calls him a ginger Irishman; distinguish that note from in-world biography.

**Sources:** [04 Maguro](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Maguro.md); [15 Klein](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/15%20Klein.md); [17 Vivaine](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/17%20Vivaine.md); [34 Rescue](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/34%20Rescue.md); [36 Intro Prevo](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/36%20Intro%20Prevo.md); [37 (Shuttle)](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/37%20(Shuttle).md); [40 Pilatus](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/40%20Pilatus.md).

### Supreme Chancellor Oron Prevo

**Role:** Notosian ruler supported by Tokusha enforcement and Mother Eye’s public narrative.

**Methods and relationships:** Pursues Keraunos, exploits Ursza’s conditioning, and attacks the faction leadership. His own account describes the constraints preventing her from harming him or Talbot; treat that explanation as his claim rather than omniscient diagnosis.

**Written trajectory:** Appears personally after the massacre; survives the balcony missile strike and leaves with Ursza. Later broadcasts stage executions of already-dead faction leaders.

**Evidence caution:** Public benevolence, popularity, and official events are filtered through Mother Eye. His claims of omniscience should not become objective character capabilities.

**Sources:** [04 Maguro](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Maguro.md); [36 Intro Prevo](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/36%20Intro%20Prevo.md); [37 (Shuttle)](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/37%20(Shuttle).md).

### Mother Eye

**Role:** Pervasive Notosian information/surveillance authority treated as an active character; disseminates ‘corrections’ and controls public accounts.

**Actions:** Names Clay as a suspect, redirects faction hostility, apparently guides him toward Harba City, and fabricates the execution broadcast. Characters’ deductions about particular interventions remain deductions.

**Relationships:** Privately converses with Halferne as ‘Professor’ and offers to protect Ursza and neutralize Keraunos. Public service to Prevo does not disclose the full extent of her independent agenda.

**Scope limitation:** Perfidy does not establish the later books’ deeper origin revelation. Do not import that biography into this book’s evidence.

**Sources:** [10 Pursuit](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/10%20Pursuit.md); [18 Scrub](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/18%20Scrub.md); [29 Norinaga](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/29%20Norinaga.md); [37 (Shuttle)](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/37%20(Shuttle).md); [39 Hello, Mother](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Hello%2C%20Mother.md).

### Markus Klein

**Role and appearance:** Head of Jade Shield; has a cybernetic arm. Operates a coastal hilltop estate/temple and a lethal pilgrimage-selection system.

**Beliefs and capabilities:** Presents intellectual/cultural subversion, black markets, and Norinaga University as his faction’s strategy. Quotations and spiritual language accompany ruthless killing; these are his professed beliefs, not an assurance of benevolence.

**Relationships:** Former associate of Obryn; acquires Keraunos through Talbot. Prudence selects/guides pilgrims for his community. Kills Marshal Harris with a concealed dart.

**Evidence caution:** Acknowledges three pilgrimage deaths and says he spared Nappal. Subsequent reports of violence around his compound do not by themselves establish a precise personal fate.

**Sources:** [04 Maguro](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Maguro.md); [13 Prudence](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/13%20Prudence.md); [14 Ascent](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/14%20Ascent.md); [15 Klein](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/15%20Klein.md); [16 Warning](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/16%20Warning.md).
