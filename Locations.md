# Locations

## Initial scope: Notosia in The Halferne Perfidy

Started 2026-10-04. This first pass covers only locations placed on Notosia and referenced in the current **Halferne Perfidy** manuscript. It does not add sites from other books. Named locations are followed by significant unnamed story sites; descriptions of unnamed sites are reference labels, not new canonical names. Parent-place relationships are marked where the prose leaves them uncertain. Author footnotes are kept distinct from names appearing in prose.

- [ ] Decide whether **Nilotpalam Shrine** should remain an author-note name or enter the prose. Keep it distinct from the white-lotus Jizo shrine.

- [ ] Distinguish the two hotels, the two dex workspaces, and the Omari office from the unlocated interrogation room.

## Scope exclusions

Excipio Gate Station is off-world, not on Notosia’s surface, so it is outside this requested list. Midway Station, Mount Pilatus, Adad’s Gu’Senlin temple, and other off-world/Earth references are also excluded. **Red Mountain** occurs within the organization name Avengers of Red Mountain; that alone does not establish a physical Notosian mountain location. Ships and transports remain transport entries rather than fixed locations. Generic unnamed apartments, stalls, corridors, and transient parking spots are not individually indexed.
