# Halferne Series: World Encyclopedia

> Scope: shared reference for the twelve Halferne novels. Coverage includes the drafted text of *The Halferne Deception* (reviewed 2026-10-02) and the revised *The Halferne Perfidy* manuscript (technology added 2026-10-06); other books have not yet been incorporated. The original Deception entries refer to files in [03-The-Halferne-Deception](https://github.com/djaysnider/03-The-Halferne-Deception), not outline chapter numbers. Future entries should identify both the book repository and source file. These are living reference notes, not a declaration that every first-draft detail is final canon. Character claims, author notes, interpretations, and unresolved conflicts are labeled separately. No facts from other books or unapproved brainstorming have been imported.

## Evidence Labels

* **Demonstrated:** an event occurs in narrated action.
* **Reported:** a character, broadcast, or briefing claims it.
* **Author note:** an out-of-story intention or explanation.
* **Unresolved:** the draft conflicts, stops short, or does not explain the mechanism.

A demonstrated capability does not establish unlimited reach. A character's confidence does not establish a universal law.

## Arco Korab and Social Structure

**Demonstrated / narrated**

* Multi-story residential and commercial levels are stacked within a kilometer-scale arcology.
* Vertical transit shafts connect levels; lateral routes branch into them.
* Rik's Level Seven home is modest; top-level residences offer luxury and open sky.
* Shift changes and circadian lighting organize daily life.

**Reported / described**

* The lower three levels offer public food and cold dorms supporting minimum subsistence.
* Aptitude assessments influence available careers and grades.
* Auria regulates access to advanced foreign technology.

**Limits and questions**

* Level Seven and Level Nine are described as broadly similar despite status assumptions.
* Arco levels and individual building floors are separate coordinate systems.
* Numerical floor layouts around Vantaris change between briefing, reconnaissance, and execution. Do not draw a definitive map yet.

**Sources:** 07, 08, 11, 12, 14, 18.

## Repulsor Transports

**Demonstrated**

* Vehicles move through vertical shafts and open air, with manual or automated piloting.
* Autoparking and emergency landing can remove much of the routine pilot workload.
* Maintenance haulers can carry other transports using a gravbeam.
* Different models have mechanical vulnerabilities that technical specialists can exploit.

**Specific rule**

* Rik explains that the “'74 Quinn Starlight” design uses one shared regulator valve.
* He deliberately increases input and miscalibrates monitoring so the regulator overheats at a calculated point.
* He later restores the vehicle quickly.

**Limits**

* This is a particular model's vulnerability, not a universal exploit.
* No full dating system is established by model abbreviations such as '74, '22, or '64.

**Sources:** 03 Clerc; 04 Mechanic; 11 Assemble; 15 Rik and Poppy.

## Gravplates, Fall Protection, and Lane Skipping

**Demonstrated**

* Gravplates compensate for much vehicle inertia but do not eliminate vertigo or all abrupt momentum effects.
* Lane skipping uses moving vehicles and infrastructure as stepping stones.
* Krisp falls when a landing goes wrong.
* Safety beams briefly catch him and release; the gravnet flickers out; he dies.

**Reported / narrated rules**

* Arco safety systems normally identify falling objects above a mass threshold.
* High falls should allow time for detection, repulsor intervention, and a final gravnet.
* Safe capture can still be painful; short falls may cause serious but treatable injuries.

**Limits and questions**

* These safeguards are fall protection, not invulnerability.
* Krisp attributes their failure to Daria; her exact access path and control over public safety systems are not independently explained.
* Distinguish vehicle gravplates, local repulsor beams, and the bottom-of-shaft gravnet.

**Sources:** 08 Rik and Lora; 22 Krisp's End; 23 Betrayal.

## Transponders and Surveillance

**Demonstrated**

* Vehicle identity systems and vidcams support tracking.
* Poppy disables a transponder while the engine remains functional.
* Bethir associates Bix, Daria, and Krisp through surveillance images from earlier events.
* Tiron warns that reuse of a surveillance vehicle can expose its owner.

**Reported**

* Rik expects disabling a transponder to require substantial work and specialized equipment.
* Poppy knows a quicker method.
* A pilot-grade transponder appears to affect tolerated vehicle operation.

**Limits**

* Turning off the transponder does not establish invisibility from cameras or other sensors.
* Broad camera coverage is described as a threat; perfect detection is not demonstrated.

**Sources:** 14, 15, 18.

## Active and Passive Scanning

**Demonstrated**

* The crew uses scans to study building layout, emissions, movement, and machinery.
* Passive reconnaissance compares received emissions with supplied plans.
* Rik records furnishings and escape-route conditions.
* Portable scanners identify components in the stolen machine.

**Reported constraints**

* Active scans risk being noticed by Vantaris' monitoring.
* Powered-down defensive equipment may not be obvious.
* Scans do not replace accurate intelligence about the nature of a security intelligence.

**Sources:** 14 Tiron and Alayn; 15 Rik and Poppy; 21 Safe House.

## Biometric Locks and Access

**Demonstrated**

* Palm-based access secures homes and the shop.
* Madhavi's security arch admits expected crew members automatically.
* Daria uses Mitchell's terminal to reach Vantaris systems.

**Reported rules**

* Rik proposes replacing an existing authorized biometric entry rather than expanding the credential store.
* Credential editing depends on access location, timing, and current authorization.
* Mitchell's terminal has exceptional privilege.

**Limits**

* Biometrics do not imply that every access system has the same configuration or weakness.
* How Mitchell is made to authorize access is not fully drafted.

**Sources:** 07, 09, 11, 12, 17–18.

## Earth Neural Augmentation

**Demonstrated**

* Daria has a neural jack behind her left ear and uses a physical connector.
* Krisp's adapter permits rapid bidirectional interaction with local systems.
* Incoming data can overwhelm her briefly.
* Disconnecting or destroying the physical adapter breaks that particular connection.

**Reported**

* Installation required precise neural mapping and SI surgery.
* Daria says she obtained legitimate technology on Earth at 19.
* Adjustment caused initial discomfort.
* Augmentation carries social stigma on Auria.

**Limits**

* Daria remains biologically human; faster access does not guarantee she can outcompute an SI.
* Her implant does not itself establish unrestricted remote network access.

**Sources:** 12 The Ask; 13 Krisp and Daria; 18 Into Vantaris; 20 Escape.

## Subconscious Memory Implants (SMI)

**Reported and demonstrated application**

* Daria describes implanted mathematical skills that operate without conscious comprehension.
* She applies them quickly to encryption and interface work.
* She wants education that would let her understand the abilities she already uses.

**Limits**

* An available skill routine is distinct from explicit explanatory knowledge.
* No general rule about personality changes or transfer of consciousness is established here.

**Sources:** 13 Krisp and Daria; 16 Rehearsal.

## Krisp's Neural Interface Adapter

**Demonstrated**

* Translates Daria's implant output into instructions for a generic local interface.
* Supports return data, text exchange, and system commands.
* Successfully tested in Madhavi's workshop and used at Vantaris.
* Bethir destroys an interface at Tiron's instruction.

**Reported design**

* Krisp predicts operation twenty to thirty times faster than ordinary spoken or typed commands.
* He explicitly excludes full sensory immersion because he lacks the needed technology and detailed neural scan.

**Limits**

* Successful integration relies on both endpoints already functioning.
* Which replacement/interface Daria possesses in 23 Betrayal after destruction in 20 Escape is not explained.

**Sources:** 13; 18; 20; 23.

## The Phrame

**Reported by Daria**

* An Earth environment allowing extensive alteration of simulated bodies and experiences.
* Can also provide telepresence access to physical locations.
* Permanent participation may involve corporate custody/support of the biological body.
* Daria found leaving difficult but did leave.

**Limits**

* Her experience and opinions are not a complete neutral account of Earth's society.
* Characters disagree over whether related technology is banned or restricted on Auria.
* No complete legal framework is supplied.

**Sources:** 12 The Ask; 13 Krisp and Daria.

## Synthetic Intelligence and Bethir / Beithir

**Demonstrated**

* Bethir challenges inconsistent security procedures.
* Overrides systems, locates identities through records, directs drones, and disables lift safeguards.
* Accepts a false trusted identity and executes Tiron's instructions.
* Separate control planes mean Daria cannot command every lift.

**Reported rules**

* Daria expects SI behavior to be constrained by rules.
* Tiron and the crew describe Bethir as corrupted or rogue.
* Bix says ordinary SI is poor at security and running corrupted AI is unethical.

**Contradiction**

* 12 The Ask openly names a security SI.
* 18 Into Vantaris has the crew react as though they were briefed only about an adaptive algorithm.
* Resolve the briefing before treating their surprise as a consistent fact.

**Limits**

* Rule constraints, corruption, and accepted authority need definitions if they become central to the resolution.
* Bethir's acceptance of Dr. Hol shows an authentication weakness; it does not prove all SI are gullible.
* Spelling variants currently appear to refer to one entity.

**Sources:** 12, 14, 18, 20.

## Kudzu

**Demonstrated**

* Tiron buys a hurried identity-related preparation targeting Oskar Hol.
* Speaking as Dr. Hol allows him to direct Bethir.

**Reported**

* Vijay has access to a limited SI that can grow a good kudzu in about a week if undetected.
* Two days may produce something that fools scrutiny temporarily.

**Unresolved**

* The exact mechanism—record modification, credential fabrication, trust conditioning, or another process—is not specified.
* Do not equate the kudzu with a simple voice imitation without author confirmation.

**Sources:** 14 Tiron and Alayn; 18 Into Vantaris; 20 Escape.

## Thoughtbridge

**Briefing claims**

* Advanced, autonomous, portable Class Nine synthetic intelligence.
* Requires no network uplink.
* Core and power supply fit into a case weighing under five kilograms.
* Prototype theft is intended to delay Vantaris commercialization.

**Observed in the unresolved safehouse scene**

* Silver box approximately 18 centimeters square and six centimeters tall.
* Simple keyboard depressions and a blue display/light.
* Scans identify two QPUs, a Hadamard bank, and a Pauli engine.
* No external networking is detected.
* Messages name Krisp, warn of pursuit, and direct group movements.

**Limits and questions**

* The machine's identity, source of messages, and means of knowing outside events are not explained.
* Scanner results do not independently establish omniscience or precognition.
* The safehouse sequence's placement is unresolved.
* Author note in 12 says the macguffin has changed and the crew is not stealing what it thinks; this is a drafting intention, not an on-page revelation.

**Sources:** 12 The Ask; 21 Safe House.

## Corporate and Security Power

**Demonstrated / reported**

* Tesnica pursues lost intellectual property through investigators, lawyers, asset freezes, and confiscation.
* Tiron's rent failure follows the freeze.
* He objects that Auria's corporations should not function as government.
* Armed pursuers impersonate security during the extraction.

**Limits**

* The legal mechanism behind Tesnica's actions is not fully supplied.
* Poppy identifies reckless public firing as contrary to legitimate security rules; treat the general prohibition as her account.
* Tesnica/Zeigncorp naming changes appear in Tiron's predicament without a clear distinction.

**Sources:** 05–06; 08; 16; 22.

## The Halferne Perfidy — Technology and Devices

Added 2026-10-06 from the revised manuscript, chapters 04–44. Chapter numbering reflects the current repository after addition of the cover; it differs from the earlier character/location pass. Sources are chapter prose unless specifically marked as author notes. This section supplements the Deception entries above: repeated technology names are cross-book evidence, not newly invented separate devices. No mechanism from other novels or brainstorming is imported.

### Interstellar travel and restricted data

#### Q-gates and quantum bridges

Demonstrated/narrated: interstellar transport and data links use a network of gates. The Excipio gate’s destruction is shown in a news recording and treated by the characters as severing travel and communication. Reported: Halferne says a replacement near end can be built in months, but its remote end must travel sublight for decades; an ordinary message takes years. Limits: these are route-specific estimates, not universal construction/travel durations. No gate throughput, energy requirement, or complete topology is established.

**Sources:** [05 Maguro](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/05%20Maguro.md); [33 The Hound](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/33%20The%20Hound.md); [42 Last Word](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/42%20Last%20Word.md).

#### Keraunos Drive / Project Keraunos

The contested object is design data, not an assembled drive. The appended Nava Yātrī Shastra identifies it as a means of faster-than-light travel beyond the predetermined Q-gate network. Earlier characters describe it as a weapon or nanotech threat; those descriptions are guesses, sales claims, or incomplete knowledge. No working drive is demonstrated in Perfidy. Speed, range, power, navigation, causality rules, and exact weapon applications remain unspecified. Mother Eye’s eight-to-twelve-year development estimate is a prediction conditioned on Prevo’s choices, not a measured build time.

**Sources:** [09 Cipher](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/09%20Cipher.md); [35 Rescue](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/35%20Rescue.md); [40 Hello, Mother](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/40%20Hello%2C%20Mother.md); [43 Last Laugh](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/43%20Last%20Laugh.md); [44 (Shastra)](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/44%20(Shastra).md).

#### Datarods

Portable cylindrical data storage; the empty case’s recess is described as approximately 12 cm long and 2 cm across. Rods can be read using a terminal and contain a directory of many texts. Limits: recess measurements are not a specification for every datarod; storage capacity/interface standards are not supplied. The footnote compares rod size/shape to stuncaps; that is an author explanation.

**Sources:** [09 Cipher](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/09%20Cipher.md); [43 Last Laugh](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/43%20Last%20Laugh.md).

#### Keraunos cipher

Thin metal square with Locke Industries logo and diagrams, pictographs, letters, and numbers visible to Clay’s implant scanner. Clay initially guesses an activation key/cipher and possible molecular-level encoding; later scenes establish the cipher’s importance to decrypting the design. His sensor speculation does not confirm a molecular mechanism. Keep the cipher distinct from the encrypted data, its container’s lock, and a decrypted copy.

**Sources:** [09 Cipher](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/09%20Cipher.md); [39 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Denouement.md); [40 Hello, Mother](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/40%20Hello%2C%20Mother.md).

#### Neuro-seal / memory lock

Demonstrated: neural cable behind the ear connects user to case; Clay selects a mental image and reactivates the seal. Reported by Locke/Clay: correct recalled memory opens it; forcing it destroys contents, and death prevents unlocking. Reported by the interrogator: likely duress sensor; unconscious access fails; possible autodestruct limits repeated attempts. The proposed near-death bypass is speculative and never shown succeeding on Clay. Locke’s exact opening mechanism remains reconstructed, not witnessed.

**Sources:** [07 La Terreur](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/07%20La%20Terreur.md); [09 Cipher](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/09%20Cipher.md); [34 The End](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/34%20The%20End.md).

#### Illumination — physical and data decoys

The monks create convincing imitations from scans; Saleh describes the cipher decoy, and Clay reconstructs the datarod exchanges. Reading the rod in the final scene reveals Buddhist texts rather than the expected Keraunos file. Clay concludes the designs have been reproduced/encoded in the shastra. Limits: physical duplication, apparent data identity, decrypted content, and an operational drive are different achievements; ‘illumination’ does not establish matter replication at arbitrary precision.

**Sources:** [36 The Long Knives](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/36%20The%20Long%20Knives.md); [39 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Denouement.md); [43 Last Laugh](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/43%20Last%20Laugh.md); [44 (Shastra)](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/44%20(Shastra).md).

### Augmentation, neurochemistry, and medicine

#### Ocular implant and HUD — Perfidy evidence

Demonstrated: receives external scanner overlays, records images, looks up identities, provides low-light enhancement and telescopic magnification, reads small markings, and presents chemical warnings/biodefense status. Clay also sees temperature readings and detects a thin security laser. Karim has an artificial scanning eye that Clay removes. Limits: sensor coverage and resolution vary; Clay explicitly cannot confirm possible molecular coding.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md); [06 Scouting](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/06%20Scouting.md); [09 Cipher](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/09%20Cipher.md); [11 Pursuit](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/11%20Pursuit.md); [15 Ascent](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/15%20Ascent.md).

#### Neural jacks and linked terminals — Perfidy evidence

Physical cable behind the ear links Locke/Clay to memory locks and links dexes to terminals. Datapad streams can route to Clay’s implant; receiving a HUD stream does not prove full sensory immersion. Extends the Deception neural augmentation entry without importing Incubus’s Phrame rules.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md); [07 La Terreur](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/07%20La%20Terreur.md); [09 Cipher](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/09%20Cipher.md); [19 Scrub](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/19%20Scrub.md).

#### Biofilters / enhanced biodefenses

Narrated: lung filters scrub Excipio’s aerosol suppressant; implant reports internal toxin screening. On Tahmo Island, Clay’s HUD warns the mixed industrial contaminants will overwhelm his protection in roughly an hour; his lungs begin to burn. Limits: protection is finite and chemical/exposure-dependent, not universal immunity. Intravenous interrogation drugs affect him. The torture-chapter footnote explicitly flags a missing explanation for this interaction.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md); [10 Yishi Party](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/10%20Yishi%20Party.md); [11 Pursuit](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/11%20Pursuit.md); [19 Scrub](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/19%20Scrub.md); [34 The End](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/34%20The%20End.md).

#### Aerosol mood suppressants and antitoxins

Excipio doses the air with a tranquilizer as crowd control; Clay’s briefing/HUD describes legality under local code and cost advantages. He infers that an unaffected man has implants or a compound-specific antitoxin; that inference is not a full inventory of countermeasures. No chemical formula or standard dose supplied.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md).

#### Shard

Talbot claims an initially unobtrusive, tasteless/colorless/odorless addictive drug; after saturation, relief lasts about 24 hours, then severe nerve pain/withdrawal can lead to insanity, coma, or fatal vascular/cardiac events within the following days. These quantities are Talbot’s account, not independent clinical measurements. Ursza’s distress and temporary relief are demonstrated. Talbot claims inventor/sole-producer status; distribution through government and private coercion is reported. No cure is demonstrated.

**Sources:** [08 Hostel](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/08%20Hostel.md); [13 Shimizu](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/13%20Shimizu.md); [21 Shrine](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/21%20Shrine.md); [29 Gu’Senlin](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/29%20Gu%E2%80%99Senlin.md).

#### Yorohito / yishi conditioning

Vivaine describes altered pleasure responses, heightened endorphins, and physiological adaptation making consensual yishi experiences rewarding despite appearances. Her claims do not establish blanket consent or invulnerability. At the later attack, she becomes violently fixated; Obryn attributes this to rewiring the pleasure trigger toward pain/suffering. Berger says guild conditioning should prevent harm to others, but that protection visibly fails.

**Sources:** [07 La Terreur](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/07%20La%20Terreur.md); [10 Yishi Party](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/10%20Yishi%20Party.md); [18 Vivaine](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/18%20Vivaine.md).

#### Ursza’s coercive conditioning and Shard interaction

Distinct from the yishi conditioning application. Prevo claims Ursza cannot harm him/Talbot under conditioned prohibitions. Saleh says Shard lets her break conditioning and conditioning lets her withstand withdrawal; this is an explanatory claim, with episodes of changing agency shown in action. Halferne promises he can break it, but no successful treatment is shown. Do not treat Gu’Senlin training, chemical dependency, and imposed neural control as one mechanism.

**Sources:** [29 Gu’Senlin](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/29%20Gu%E2%80%99Senlin.md); [37 Intro Prevo](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/37%20Intro%20Prevo.md); [39 Denouement](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/39%20Denouement.md).

#### Neural leucotome; dexes / scrubs

Ren confirms Clay’s identification of a frontal-cortex device that erases memories of work performed while terminal-connected upon disconnection. Enables a privacy-oriented information trade. Later recognition of earlier dealings causes Clay to question the system. Limits: session-content memory vs broad knowledge is not completely specified; suspected government allegiance does not prove device failure. Koji has a similar device.

**Sources:** [19 Scrub](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/19%20Scrub.md); [23 The Eyes of Baal](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/23%20The%20Eyes%20of%20Baal.md); [26 Back to Ren](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/26%20Back%20to%20Ren.md); [28 The Heist](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/28%20The%20Heist.md).

#### Regen units

Reported: Vivaine has used one after a yishi session and still felt sore; Clay anticipates a couple of hours of regeneration before travel home. No treatment session is shown, and speed of recovery, eligible injuries, limits, and resurrection capability are not established.

**Sources:** [10 Yishi Party](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/10%20Yishi%20Party.md); [42 Last Word](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/42%20Last%20Word.md).

#### Nerve-induction interrogation apparatus

Demonstrated: microfiber filaments extend from attached wires to nerve receptors across major muscle groups, causing cramps and burning pain. Additional injected drugs produce euphoria and are used in a proposed fatal unlocking attempt. Clay recalls being taught the induced pain itself is not physically dangerous; that recollection does not establish all settings are safe or eliminate cramp-related injury.

**Sources:** [34 The End](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/34%20The%20End.md).

### Surveillance, interfaces, and information control

#### Portable full-spectrum scanner array

Ness’s hotel rig maps portions of La Terreur and overlays labels/data on Clay’s natural vision; occupants can be monitored. Its energy signature can attract port authorities. Extends Deception’s scanning limits: an empty result does not prove absence of a secret project, and scanned design data need not produce an unusual physical signature.

**Sources:** [06 Scouting](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/06%20Scouting.md); [07 La Terreur](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/07%20La%20Terreur.md).

#### Commercial tracking kit / miniature beacon

Karim’s commercially available kit is appropriated by Clay. The beacon can be moved between case and person and switched off; Ness follows coordinates, while Clay can identify it nearby. Limits: tracking follows the transmitter rather than authenticating the carrier or contents. No fixed range, battery life, or universal penetration rule supplied.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md); [07 La Terreur](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/07%20La%20Terreur.md); [08 Hostel](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/08%20Hostel.md); [10 Yishi Party](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/10%20Yishi%20Party.md).

#### Datapads, pocket terminals, and comm units

Portable records, briefing exchange, identity lookup, messaging, scanner control, and voice communication. Clay’s datapad emits a short-range surveillance-jamming signal in Excipio’s restroom. Limits: a jammed area may itself trigger investigation; local jamming does not erase logs or grant general network control.

Demonstrated: floating displays/credentials, neural-linked interface projections, Clay’s ring displaying his daughter, and Halferne’s remote image carried by a hovering holodrone. IDs, diplomatic credentials, and transit permissions govern movement; Halferne’s ID is checked against his bioprint. A field suppresses conversation in the lounge and drops when a participant leaves. Acoustic operation is established; blocking cameras, optical lip-reading, or network surveillance is not.

### Weapons, mobility, construction, and everyday devices

#### Khnum 210

Narrated identification: military urban-assault plasma rifle, upward of 200 bolts/minute and accuracy near 100 m. Clay judges it dangerous to station walls/utilities; the displayed rifles lack power cells and serve as intimidation props while guards’ sidearms remain armed. Values are model-specific, not generic plaser specifications.

**Sources:** [04 Excipio Station](https://github.com/djaysnider/01-The-Halferne-Perfidy/blob/main/04%20Excipio%20Station.md).

#### Stuncaps

Demonstrated at hostel: thrown cylindrical device creates a directed polysonic shockwave, pain/disorientation, and throws attackers backward. Intended as deterrent, but Clay notes close-range danger. No permanent injury observed in that use; this is not a universal safety guarantee. Same-shape-as-datarod comparison is an author footnote.

### Perfidy rule questions / punch list

* Explain why interrogation drugs bypass or overwhelm Clay’s biofilters; the author note acknowledges this gap. Keep atmospheric filtration distinct from all-purpose blood detoxification.
* Define the neuro-lock’s death, duress, and forced-opening safeguards consistently. The near-death bypass is a character’s hypothesis, not an established successful method.
* Separate Shard’s ordinary dependency effects from its unusual interaction with Ursza’s conditioning. Talbot’s ‘no real effects’ claim should not erase the later disclosed interaction.
* Clarify what Ren’s leucotome erases, what broad relationship knowledge remains, and why he can recall particular prior dealings.
* Keep a working Keraunos drive distinct from design possession, decryption, decoy rods, and shastra encoding. Perfidy supplies no test flight or quantitative engineering specification.
* Keep Q-gate replacement times tied to the Excipio route and physical delivery requirement; a quantum connection does not make the initial endpoint deployment instantaneous.
* If the existing Character Bible or Locations sources are refreshed, update their Perfidy chapter filenames to the new numbering. This technology pass does not revise those separate documents.
